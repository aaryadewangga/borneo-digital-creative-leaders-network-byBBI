A

About the Program

What is the Borneo Digital & Creative Leaders Network?

It is a five-month program for digital and creative professionals in Pontianak, Kuching, and Kota Kinabalu. You’ll learn practical SI (Super Intelligence) skills and design thinking, pick a real challenge in your city, and develop a prototype that responds to it. Along the way, you’ll connect with peers across Indonesian and Malaysian Borneo.

The program is run by the Biji-biji Initiative with support from the U.S. Embassy Jakarta and the U.S. Embassy Kuala Lumpur, and hosted by the American Corners in Pontianak, Kuching, and Kota Kinabalu.

What will I gain from joining?
  • Hands-on SI skills you can use in your work right away
  • A practical approach to understanding problems and designing solutions
  • Coaching from a mentor in your city
  • Connections with professionals across Indonesian and Malaysian Borneo
  • A chance to present your work at the Symposium and compete for implementation grants
What does the program timeline look like?
  • Applications: November 16 to December 6, 2026
  • Results announced: December 22, 2026
  • Virtual Launch and 2-Day Intensive: January 2027
  • Prototype Development: February to April 2027
  • Symposium in Pontianak: May 2027
  • Member-Led Network: from June 2027
Is there a fee? What costs are covered?

The program is free. During the 2-Day Intensive, meals are provided, but transportation and accommodation are not covered, so you’ll need to arrange your own travel to your American Corner each day. If you are selected as a finalist, your travel and accommodation for the Symposium in Pontianak are covered.

B

Eligibility

Who can apply?

The program is open to digital and creative professionals aged 22 to 40 who live in or near Pontianak, Kuching, or Kota Kinabalu and can travel to their local American Corner and back on the same day. You might be employed, freelancing, running a small business, or leading a community or initiative. You should also be able to commit to the full program from January to April 2027, including both full days of the in-person Intensive. The program is not designed for full-time students.

Do I need experience with SI tools?

No. Both beginners and experienced users are welcome.

Can I use this program to get funding for my business?

Not as the main goal. Projects should benefit a community or a local economy rather than mainly grow a private business.

C

Application and Selection

How do I apply, and when is the deadline?

Fill out the online form at [link] by December 6, 2026. You’ll choose the chapter closest to where you live. The form is in English and includes a few multiple-choice and short written questions. It takes about 25 minutes, so we suggest reading the questions first and preparing your answers before you start.

Do I need perfect English? Can I use SI tools to write my answers?

No need for perfect English. We care about what you say, not how polished it sounds. You can use SI tools for help, but your answers should reflect your own experience and ideas.

Do I need a CV or portfolio? Can I apply as a team?

A CV or portfolio is optional, but it helps us get to know you. Applications are individual, and you’ll have the chance to team up with other participants once the program starts.

How are applicants selected?

We look for people who are motivated, have experience turning ideas into something real, are close to a real challenge in their city, and want to contribute to a network of peers. About 25 to 30 participants will be selected in each city.

When will I hear back, and what if I’m on the waitlist?

Results will be sent by email on December 22, 2026. Selected participants will be asked to confirm their place, including attendance on both days of the Intensive, by December 31, 2026. If you’re on the waitlist, we’ll contact you if a place becomes available. Be sure to check your spam folder.

Can I edit my application after submitting?

No. Please review your answers carefully before you submit.

D

Learning Journey

How much time do I need to commit?

Plan for about 2 or 3 hours per week from February to April 2027. This includes monthly sessions with your mentor and peers, plus time to work on your project. In January, you’ll also join a half-day Virtual Launch and a 2-day in-person Intensive at your American Corner. Both Intensive days are full-day sessions, and attendance on both days is required.

What happens at the Virtual Launch and the 2-Day Intensive?

The Virtual Launch brings participants from all three cities together online to kick off the program. The 2-Day Intensive at your American Corner is hands-on: you’ll practice using SI tools, learn design thinking, and start shaping a project around a challenge in your city.

What are coaching clinics and cross-border exchanges, and what if I miss one?

Coaching clinics are monthly sessions with your chapter mentor to get feedback on your project. Cross-border exchanges are monthly online sessions where you meet participants from the other cities. Active participation is expected throughout the program, so please let us know in advance if you can’t attend a session.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Participants who complete the program receive a Certificate of Completion. Finalists who present at the Symposium also receive a Finalist Certificate. Completion requirements will be shared at the Virtual Launch.

E

Prototypes, Symposium and Grants

What kind of prototype and challenge should I work on?

You choose a challenge you see in your own city, work, or community, such as digital skills, education, small business, creative industries, tourism, or community services. You don’t need to build a fully working product. A prototype can be a mock-up, a workflow, a service design, a campaign idea, or a simple working version you can test with real users.

Do I work alone or in a team?

Most participants work on their own project. You can also team up with participants from the other side of the border to work on a shared challenge.

How are the finalists selected?

An expert panel selects about 30 finalists from all three cities based on the quality of their prototypes. More details will be shared during the program.

What happens at the Symposium, and who covers travel?

The Symposium is a two-day event in Pontianak in May 2027, where finalists present their projects to judges, peers, and guests. Travel and accommodation are covered for all finalists. Finalists from Malaysia will need a valid passport.

How do the implementation grants work?

At the Symposium, four projects will receive implementation grants totaling USD 4,500, including one award for a cross-border team. Grant recipients will put their projects into action from June to August 2027.

F

After the Program

What happens after the program ends?

You’ll stay on as a member of the Borneo Digital & Creative Leaders Network, a network run by its own members, with activities in each city and across the border.
